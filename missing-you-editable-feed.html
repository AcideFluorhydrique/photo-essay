Ruiqi/Rachael Tong, photo essay of PoH Fall26

Missing You Is a Part of Moving On

A personal geography of Shanghai. The photographs are the work: the image, erasure, handwriting, and legend belong together.

Photographs and legends extracted from the main essay. Short analysis is included only where it supports the image.

📷 2007 · Playground beside my uncle’s old house Main photo essay · page 3

2007 · Playground beside my uncle’s old house

Shot in 2007, at the playground beside my uncle’s old house with my cousin when she came to Shanghai. I used Procreate to digitally erase her face and write “I remember us before I learned to remember”.

The photograph holds a memory from before memory became deliberate: a childhood relationship preserved, but no longer fully accessible.

📷 2007 · The old family house Main photo essay · page 4

2007 · The old family house

Shot in 2007, at the old house of my family with my father. I used Procreate to digitally erase his face and write “your love is real, so is your abuse”.

A photograph cannot summarize an entire relationship. Affection and harm can coexist in the same history.

📷 2007 · The old family house Main photo essay · page 5

2007 · The old family house

Shot in 2007, at the old house of my family. I used Procreate to digitally erase her face and write “missing you is a part of moving on”.

A single photograph cannot summarize an entire relationship, so I find myself patching up and reconstructing the past from these pieces.

📷 2021 · Window shopping with a friend Main photo essay · page 8

2021 · Window shopping with a friend

Shot in 2021 with my friend X during window shopping. I used Procreate to digitally erase her body and wrote “best ‘counselor’ ever”.

Sometimes what we miss is not only a person, but also the version of ourselves who existed alongside them.

📷 2026 · With a friend and her boyfriend Main photo essay · page 9

2026 · With a friend and her boyfriend

Shot in 2026 with my friend N and her boyfriend. I used Procreate to digitally erase their hands and wrote “maybe I miss you, maybe I miss who I was with you”.

The absence invites a question: am I remembering the person, or the self I was with them?

📷 2023 · A small street in Shanghai Main photo essay · page 10

2023 · A small street in Shanghai

Shot in 2023 when I caught a beetle in a small street in Shanghai with my friend K. I digitally erased their hand and wrote “we used to be us. We are no longer us. We won’t be us any more”.

Relationships change even when a photograph keeps one moment intact.

📷 2025 · Playing Cyberpunk 2077 Main photo essay · page 11

2025 · Playing Cyberpunk 2077

Shot in 2025 with my friend J when we were playing Cyberpunk 2077. I used Procreate to digitally erase her hand and wrote “Somewhere in my memory, we are still playing”.

Like the nostalgia in Severance, a remembered scene can become a place we revisit even when the present has moved on.

📷 2024 · My dojo, first two stripes in Brazilian jiu-jitsu Main photo essay · page 12

2024 · My dojo, first two stripes in Brazilian jiu-jitsu

Shot in 2024 in my dojo in memory of my first two stripes in Brazilian jiu-jitsu. My friends were doing odd postures and chokeholds to me as a celebration. I digitally erased their faces and wrote “oss!”—an etiquette expression in many martial arts representing respect, gratitude and understanding.

Absence here does not erase the shared celebration; the gesture remains.

📷 2007 & 2026 · Sundial, Century Avenue Main photo essay · page 13

2007 & 2026 · Sundial, Century Avenue

Left: shot in 2007 at the sundial tourist attraction on Century Avenue with my cousin. I used Procreate to digitally erase his face and wrote “something old, something new”. Right: shot on October 17, 2026, at the same attraction from a different perspective.

The place remains recognisable, but the people and the person looking at it have changed.
